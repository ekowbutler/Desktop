Origins and education

From Ezinlibo to the world

Peter Augustine Blay comes from Ezinlibo, in Ghana’s Western Region. His schooling ran from Accra to Sandhurst, through infantry and commando training in India, and on to staff and war college courses in Ghana and the United States.

Learning across continents

His ties to home have stayed constant through a career of national and international service.
