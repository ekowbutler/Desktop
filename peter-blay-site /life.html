The person behind the uniform

Grounded in faith, close to community

A devout Catholic, he carried a spirit of service from his early days as a CYO banner bearer into a lifetime of parish and community involvement.

Parish and community

He has served as a chorister and on the Parish Pastoral Council at Holy Spirit Cathedral, Accra. Today he sits on the National Board of the Society of St Vincent de Paul, is a patron to Catholic societies, and is a Noble Knight of the Knights of St John International.

In Ezinlibo he has supported improvements at St Matthew Catholic Church, including reconstruction of the Mission House.

Outside work he enjoys table tennis, jogging and gardening.
