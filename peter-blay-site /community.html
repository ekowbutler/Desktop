Giving back to home

Progress is a shared effort

In Nzemaland, service has also meant making opportunity tangible: through education, community infrastructure and support for the next generation.

Projects

01

Learning spaces

With “Gen Blay and Friends”, he supported administration and library and ICT blocks at Half Assini Senior High School and Annor Adjaye Senior High School, Ezinlibo.

02

Opportunity for students

A trust fund supports bright but needy students across the three Nzema Districts, including talented students studying for tertiary education in Ghana and abroad.

03

Land for a College of Education

He donated 12 acres of land at Ezinlibo to support the Government of Ghana’s plans for a College of Education. He has also helped Nzema youth into employment placements.
