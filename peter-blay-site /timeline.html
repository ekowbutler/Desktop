Across the years

A life in milestones

A selection of moments from a career in uniform, diplomacy and community service.

Milestones

  1. 1972

    Royal Military Academy Sandhurst

    Begins a course of professional military education.

    Military
  2. 1976

    Infantry and Commando Schools, Belgaum

    Continues specialist training in India.

    Military
  3. 1985

    Ghana Armed Forces Command and Staff College

    Completes staff college training in Ghana.

    Military
  4. 2000

    Establishes the African Union Liaison Mission

    As Africa’s Senior Military Representative, establishes AULMEE in Ethiopia and Eritrea.

    Diplomacy
  5. 2006–09

    Defence Adviser in Abuja

    Serves at the Ghana High Commission in Nigeria.

    Diplomacy
  6. 2009

    Chief of the Defence Staff

    Appointed to lead the Ghana Armed Forces.

    Military
  7. 2011

    International and national recognition

    Inducted into the U.S. Army War College International Fellows Hall of Fame and recognised by IMANI Ghana and the West African Nobles Forum.

    Recognition
  8. 2014–17

    Ambassador to Côte d’Ivoire

    Represents Ghana in the Republic of Côte d’Ivoire.

    Diplomacy
  9. 2015

    Companion of the Order

    Receives a State honour from President John Dramani Mahama.

    Recognition
  10. 2025

    Interim National Security Coordination Committee

    Appointed a member to oversee security pending substantive appointments.

    Recognition
