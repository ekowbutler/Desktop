Service in uniform

Leadership at every level

A career built in the field, in training and at the highest level of command, culminating in his appointment as Chief of the Defence Staff.

Roles

Command

Aide de Camp to the Army Commander. Commanding Officer, Jungle Warfare Training School.

Training

Directing Staff at GAFCSC. Chief Instructor, Army Combat Training School.

Administration

Director General, Personnel Administration. Chief of Staff, General Headquarters.

Chief of the Defence Staff

Appointed in March 2009 by President John Evans Atta Mills, leading the Ghana Armed Forces through 2013.

Institutions and projects linked to his time in the Armed Forces

  • Pension House
  • GAF Sports Complex

  • Defence Industrial Holding Company
