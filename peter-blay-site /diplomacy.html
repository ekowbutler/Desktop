Beyond Ghana

Service on the world stage

His work in international missions and diplomacy connected Ghana’s armed forces with partners across Africa and beyond.

Postings

2000

Peace missions and the AU liaison mission

Served with United Nations missions in Lebanon and Cambodia. As Africa’s Senior Military Representative to the Military Coordination Commission, he established the African Union Liaison Mission in Ethiopia and Eritrea (AULMEE) in December 2000.

2006–09

Defence Adviser, Abuja

Served at the Ghana High Commission in Abuja, Nigeria.

2014–17

Ambassador to Côte d’Ivoire

Ghana’s Ambassador to the Republic of Côte d’Ivoire, from July 2014 to January 2017.
